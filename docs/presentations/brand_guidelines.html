Brand Identity

Brand Name: Sagar Fresh (Tagline: Farm to Fork Fresh)

Aesthetic Benchmark: Licious-Grade Premium Freshness. Clean white/gray canvas with bold crimson accents and clinical food safety transparency.

Core Tone: Authentic, appetizing, hygienic, zero-hype, and environmentally accountable.

Strict Anti-Vibecoding Rule

• NO neon/harsh radial gradient backgrounds.
• NO dark crypto/cyberpunk UI themes.
• NO liquid glassmorphism or floating translucent orbs.
• YES: High-contrast crisp typography, real raw meat/fish photography, and transparent Net/Gross weight labeling.

Font Weight Usage Rules
900 / 800 — Black / Extrabold

Reserved strictly for prices, hero headlines, and primary brand badges. Delivers instant visual anchor.

700 — Bold

Used on Product Titles, action buttons, category names, and section labels.

400 — Regular

Used on product cut descriptions, origin jetty info, and disclaimers to ensure comfortable reading.
