Industry Challenges & The GoMeat Solution

01

The Frozen Meat Illusion

MARKET DEFECT: Standard delivery apps deliver rock-frozen cuts with broken cold-chains. Ice crystallization punctures meat cell membranes, leaching vital juices and flavor upon defrosting.
GOMEAT SOLUTION: 0°C to 4°C active chilled cold chain dispatched from micro-hubs in under 90 minutes. Fresh cut daily, never frozen.
02

Single-Use Styrofoam Waste

ENVIRONMENTAL CRISIS: Every conventional meat delivery generates 1-2 kg of toxic thermocol (EPS) packaging and plastic bubble wrap that clogs municipal landfills for over 500 years.
GOMEAT SOLUTION: ThermalVault™ reusable EPP insulated boxes with frozen gel packs. ₹250 refundable deposit credited back to UPI in 1 click upon box return.
03

Insecure & Tampered Checkouts

COMMERCE VULNERABILITY: Most amateur stores trust frontend-submitted prices, use fake simulated payments, or static QR codes prone to user tampering and chargeback losses.
GOMEAT SOLUTION: Tamper-proof server-side price calculation from MongoDB, HMAC SHA-256 signatures, and cryptographic raw-body webhook verification.

Payment Engineering

Module 1: Real Razorpay Payment Gateway Integration

Enterprise Payment Requirements Met

1. Server-Side Price Calculation: Prices are calculated strictly by querying MongoDB product records. Frontend submitted totals are discarded to prevent inspect-element tampering.
2. HMAC SHA-256 Signature Verification: Crypto engine computes HMAC SHA-256 over (razorpay_order_id + '|' + razorpay_payment_id) with RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET to guarantee authentic bank confirmation.
3. Raw Body Webhook Verification: Preserved express raw body for raw byte-matching against x-razorpay-signature, supporting captured, failed, and refund events.
4. Idempotency & Duplicate Prevention: Re-verifying an already PAID order returns 200 OK immediately with the existing order, preventing duplicate records or multiple debits.

Cryptographic Payment Flow

1

Backend looks up cuts in DB, validates stock, adds ₹250 deposit, and calls Razorpay SDK to generate order_id in paise.

Dynamic checkout.js launches official Razorpay modal. User completes payment via GPay, PhonePe, Cards, or NetBanking.

3
POST /api/payments/verify

Client submits razorpay_order_id, razorpay_payment_id, and signature. Node.js crypto engine authenticates HMAC SHA-256 signature.

4
Atomic Persistence & Stock Deduction

Decrements product inventory in MongoDB, locks cold-box asset serial number, and transitions orderStatus to CONFIRMED.
