Industry Context & Market Friction
Slide 2: Critical Industry Challenges
02 / 12
1. Cold-Chain Temperature Breaches
  • Traditional wet markets operate at ambient temperatures (28°C–34°C), accelerating bacterial growth and moisture deterioration within 45 minutes of inward.
  • Existing online delivery platforms use standard cardboard boxes with small ice gel packs that thaw during metro traffic delays (65–85 minutes).
  • Core package temperatures routinely spike above 7°C, compromising freshness without customer visibility.
2. Packaging Cost & Waste Drain
  • Market incumbents spend between ₹38 and ₹55 per delivery on single-use thermocol linings, foam sheets, and bubble wrap.
  • This cost represents dead capital burned on every fulfillment cycle.
  • Creates substantial municipal landfill waste, alienating environmentally conscious urban millennial and Gen-Z households.
3. Zero Verification & Trust Deficit
  • Current brands market products as "chilled, never frozen" but provide zero proof to the consumer upon arrival.
  • Consumers frequently experience water-logged meat from thawed ice or chemical odor from formalin-treated seafood.
  • High return rates and customer churn due to inconsistent freshness across seasonal heatwaves.
Economic Impact of Current Industry Inefficiencies

Incumbents suffer an estimated 4.8% customer refund rate due to temperature degradation in transit and spend over 7.2% of net revenue exclusively on non-recoverable disposable packaging materials.

Proprietary Solution & Operating Architecture
Slide 3: The Sagar Fresh Solution — FreshSeal™
03 / 12
1. Precision Harbor-Side Inward & Blast Chilling

Daily catches inward from Mangalore and Ratnagiri jetties by 4:00 AM. Products are descaled, washed in RO-purified water, portioned into neat cuts, and immediately blast-chilled to 0°C–2°C within 30 minutes, preserving cellular muscle texture.

2. ThermalVault™ Reusable Cold-Gel Boxes

Constructed from high-density Expanded Polypropylene (EPP) with food-grade phase-change gel plates. Maintains core temperature below 4°C for up to 36 continuous hours without external power.

3. Real-Time Consumer IoT Telemetry

Each container features an integrated digital thermal sensor. Customers track live internal box temperatures on their web application in real time during transit (0°C to 4°C safety gauge).

4. Automated Deposit & Return Engine

A refundable deposit of ₹250 is included at checkout. Upon scheduled collection of the empty container by return couriers, ₹250 is instantly credited back to the customer's UPI account via automated webhook.

10 / 12
Technology Stack Overview
  • Frontend: React 19 Single Page Application with Tailwind CSS v4, dynamic catalog drawers, and mobile-responsive viewport.
  • Backend API: Node.js & Express 4 RESTful architecture with secure JSON Web Token (JWT) authentication and role-based access control.
  • Cloud Database: MongoDB Atlas cloud cluster with Mongoose ODM schemas for users, products, orders, coolers, and return tracking.
  • Cloud Deployment: Vercel Serverless environment with automatic edge asset distribution and dynamic API rewrites.
Data Entities & IoT Monitoring
  • Container Fleet Telemetry: Each cooler record stores RFID serials, battery charge, sanitation logs, and lifetime cycle wear.
  • Live Probe Engine: Ingests periodic temperature measurements into the order document, generating real-time audit graphs on consumer devices.
  • Automated Refund Webhook: Triggers deposit balance updates and generates UPI transaction receipts upon operational collection verification.
